Demo calculation:
  L = largest eligible worker allocatable CPU = 12000m
  L / 2 = 6000m
  q = 6100m
  q > L / 2: 6100m > 6000m
  q <= smallest free requested CPU: 6100m <= 11300m
  eligible workers = 3
  requested replicas = eligible workers + 1 = 4
